Founder letter

Deju Studio was built around one idea: that a manicure is finished work, not a quick errand.

I'm Desty Zulfa Nabila, founder and the artist behind every set. I trained in russian manicure technique, refined my structured gel and extensions practice through Colorpot and Carissa Nails, and opened Deju Studio in Ubud in April 2025 as a private space for clients who want their nails treated with the same care a tailor gives a hem.

The studio is small on purpose. One client at a time, one set at a time. I work in clean, minimalist finishes for clients who want their hands to disappear; in structured gel and BIAB for clients who want longevity; and in hand-painted nail art and 4D detailing for clients who want a small piece of art they get to wear for a few weeks.

Hygiene is non-negotiable. Every tool is sterilised between clients. Files and buffers are single-use. I keep the room calm and the consultation honest, so you leave with a set that suits your hands and your week, not whatever was trending on Instagram that morning.

If something here speaks to you, send me a message. I reply by WhatsApp the same day.

- Desty

Philosophy

Hand-finished, every time.

Russian manicure technique gives the cleanest cuticle line, and a structured gel apex gives the longest wear. Both take longer than a standard set. We don't shortcut either.

Specialisations

From minimalist to 4D.

  • Russian manicure (advanced cuticle technique)
  • Structured gel and BIAB
  • Soft tip / Gel-X, polygel, sculpture extensions
  • Hand-painted luxury nail art (basic to 4D)
  • Nail correction and safe removal
  • Nail health assessment

Sense of place

Ubud, on Gn. Abang Street.

Lodtunduh sits south of central Ubud, where the rice paddies start. The studio is set back from the street, framed by the same banyan and palm that grew here long before any of this. You'll hear birds, occasionally a scooter, mostly silence.
