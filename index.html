Philosophy

Hand-finished, every time.

Russian manicure technique gives the cleanest cuticle line, and a structured gel apex gives the longest wear. Both take longer than a standard set. We don't shortcut either.

Specialisations

From minimalist to 4D.

  1. Russian manicure (advanced cuticle technique)
  2. Structured gel and BIAB
  3. Soft tip / Gel-X, polygel, sculpture extensions
  4. Hand-painted luxury nail art (basic to 4D)
  5. Nail correction and safe removal
  6. Nail health assessment
