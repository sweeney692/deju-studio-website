The artist

Meet Desty.

Deju Studio is the work of Desty Zulfa Nabila, a Bali-based nail artist who founded the studio to deliver the slow, hand-finished service that ensures every detail is captured in her nail art. Desty trained in nail artistry through Colorpot's basic-to-professional nail course and Carissa Nails' Nail Essential Online Course, building a practice rooted in precision, meticulous cuticle work, and balanced nail structure.

Her signature is a Russian manicure paired with structured gel - the cleanest cuticle line a manicure can give, with the longest wear a gel set can hold. The work spans clean minimalist sets, soft tip and Gel-X extensions, polygel and sculpture, and detailed hand-painted nail art from a single accent up to fully sculpted 4D pieces.

Deju Studio is private and appointment-only, serving Indonesian residents and the international clients who travel through Ubud and the wider Gianyar Regency. Desty consults on every appointment to match style, shape, length, and finish to the client's lifestyle and event.

Questions

Good things to know.

How much does a manicure cost at Deju Studio?

A standard manicure with Russian manicure technique and a vitamin treatment starts at 150,000 IDR. A spa manicure with hand massage is 200,000 IDR. Gel manicure is 250,000 IDR. Hard gel manicure is 300,000 IDR. BIAB manicure (Builder In A Bottle, which strengthens the natural nail) is 400,000 IDR.

How much does a gel manicure cost in Ubud?

A gel manicure at Deju Studio is 250,000 IDR. This includes a Russian manicure to prepare the nail, shaping, a single colour or glitter gel application, and a vitamin treatment. Hard gel and BIAB manicures, which last longer and add structure, are 300,000 and 400,000 IDR respectively.

What is BIAB and is it worth the upgrade?

BIAB stands for "Builder In A Bottle" - a flexible gel applied over the natural nail to add strength and a slight apex without the bulk of hard gel or extensions. It is the right choice if your natural nails are thin or peeling and you want to grow them out, but you do not need full extensions. BIAB lasts longer than a standard gel manicure and is gentler on the natural nail than hard gel. At Deju Studio a BIAB manicure is 400,000 IDR.

What is the difference between Soft Tip, Gel-X, polygel, and sculpture extensions?

All four are ways to add length and shape to the natural nail. Soft Tip and Gel-X are pre-shaped tips applied on the natural nail with gel - fastest to apply and ideal for a uniform shape. Polygel is a hybrid gel-acrylic applied using forms, which lets the artist sculpt a custom apex and width. Sculpture extensions are built directly on the natural nail with gel and forms, giving the most natural look and the most control over the shape. Pricing is 400,000 (Soft Tip / Gel-X), 450,000 (Tip Overlay), 500,000 (Polygel), and 600,000 (Sculpture) IDR.

What does "nail art level 1 to 5" mean?

Deju Studio prices nail art in five tiers by complexity. Level 1 is simple linework or a single accent. Level 2 is multiple elements per nail or full-set linework. Level 3 is detailed hand-painted designs across the full set with layering, chrome, or intricate line work. Level 4 introduces three-dimensional sculpted detail, raised florals, or mixed media. Level 5 is full 4D sculpted artistry with complex three-dimensional motifs across the set. Pricing runs from 200,000 IDR (Lv. 1) to 700,000 IDR (Lv. 5).

Do you remove gel or extensions safely?

Yes. Gel and extension removal is done with a gentle e-file and soak-off, never by lifting or peeling. Removal is priced 100,000 to 150,000 IDR depending on what is being removed (regular gel, hard gel, or full extensions). If you arrive with damaged or lifting work from another studio, removal can be combined with a nail health assessment and a corrective application.

Can I bring a reference photo for nail art?

Yes - reference photos are encouraged. Send your inspiration over WhatsApp when you book and Desty will confirm what's possible and which art level the design falls into.

Do I need to book in advance, or do you take walk-ins?

Deju Studio is appointment only and does not accept walk-ins. Each appointment is given the time the service requires - a Russian manicure and a gel manicure together can take 90 minutes or more - so the schedule is planned in advance. Bookings are confirmed by WhatsApp.

How long does a gel manicure take?

A gel manicure with Russian manicure preparation, shaping, gel application, and a vitamin treatment typically takes 90 minutes. Hard gel and BIAB manicures take a little longer because of the additional gel layers. Nail art is added on top of the base time according to the level.

How long does a full set of extensions take?

A full set of extensions with a colour or simple finish typically takes two to three hours, depending on the extension type (Soft Tip / Gel-X is faster than sculpture) and the complexity of the shape. Nail art layered on top adds further time according to the level chosen.

What hygiene and sterilisation standards do you follow?

All metal tools are sterilised between clients. Consumables (files, buffers, single-use bits) are single-use only. Workstations and surfaces are wiped down after every appointment. Russian manicure technique itself is non-invasive on healthy cuticles.

Are your products cruelty-free or low-tox?

Deju Studio works with professional gel and BIAB brands selected for quality and skin-safety. If you have specific allergies or sensitivities, mention them when you book and we'll match the products to you.

Do you do bridal nails in Bali?

Yes. Deju Studio offers bridal-suitable manicures, extensions, and hand-painted nail art at every art level, including 3D and 4D sculpted designs that suit wedding aesthetics. Book ahead and bring a reference - bridal sets are typically booked over WhatsApp with extra time blocked off.
